---

### ✅ **How to Practice Sparklines in Power BI**

1️⃣ **Open Power BI Desktop**
Make sure you have a simple table with **some date and measure columns**, for example:

* *Region*
* *Year*
* *Sales*
* *12 Month % Change*

---

2️⃣ **Create a Table Visual**

* Add *Region* and *12 Month % Change* to the table.

---

3️⃣ **Add a Sparkline**

* Right-click the *12 Month % Change* column in the table.
* Click **Add a Sparkline**.
* Set **Y-axis** to *12 Month % Change*.
* Set **X-axis** to *Year*.
* Click **Create** — now each region row will show its own tiny trend line!

---

4️⃣ **Format the Sparkline**

* Click the table visual.
* In the **Visualizations pane**, go to **Sparklines** settings.
* Change **Line Color**, **Width**, and **Add Markers** (like for negative points).

---

5️⃣ **Add a Second Visual (Optional)**

* Add a normal **Line Chart** to compare.
* Put *Year* on the X-axis and *12 Month % Change* on the Y-axis.
* Format it to show the trend more clearly.

---

6️⃣ **Explore & Play**

* Try changing the sparkline type to **Column** instead of Line.
* Add more measures if you like.
* Practice adding **markers** for highest or lowest points.
