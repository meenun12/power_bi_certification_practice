---

### 📌 **1️⃣ Why use text boxes, images, and shapes?**

* They help **make your report clearer** for people who use it.
* For example, you can add instructions so users know how to interact with visuals.
* They **don’t show data**, but they make your report **easier to read and use**.

---

### 📌 **2️⃣ How to add a text box**

* Go to the **Home tab → Insert → Text box**.
* A text box appears on the page — you type your message, e.g. *“Click on a year to focus on this year.”*
* The text box works like a mini Word document:

  * You can change font size, color, bold, italic, underline, alignment.
  * You can change the **background color**, **text color**, and add a **border**.
  * You can resize and move it anywhere on the page.

---

### 📌 **3️⃣ How to format a text box**

* Use the **toolbar** that pops up when you type.
* There is no font size slider in the side **Format pane**, so you use the toolbar.
* You can add borders so it’s easy to see.
* Add instructions, short tips, or even hyperlinks.

---

### 📌 **4️⃣ Add shapes (like arrows, circles, etc.)**

* Go to **Home → Insert → Shapes**.
* Pick a shape (for example, an arrow to point at a chart).
* Resize and rotate it so it points correctly.
* In the **Format Shape pane**, you can:

  * Rotate it to any angle.
  * Change the color of the line and fill.
  * Add a title or text inside it.
  * Add shadow or glow effects.
  * Adjust corners and line style.

---

### 📌 **5️⃣ Link shapes to visuals**

* Use shapes to **guide users’ eyes** — for example, an arrow pointing from your instruction text to a chart.
* This makes it clearer what they should click or look at.

---

### 📌 **6️⃣ Extra formatting for shapes**

* Change shape type (arrow, rectangle, circle, etc.).
* Add text inside shapes.
* Adjust padding so text is not too close to the edge.
* Some shapes have special options like arrowhead size.

---

### 📌 **7️⃣ Add images**

* Go to **Home → Insert → Image**.
* Pick an image from your computer. (You **cannot** link directly from a website.)
* Examples: company logo, icons, screenshots.
* You can **lock the aspect ratio** — this keeps the image from getting stretched.
* You can’t add a clickable link to an image, but later you’ll learn how to add an **action** to an image to make it do something.

---

### 📌 **8️⃣ Add hyperlinks in text boxes**

* Highlight a word in your text box → insert link.
* Example: *“Full instructions — click **here**.”*
* In Power BI Desktop, the link won’t open — but once you publish your report, it will work for users.

---

### 📌 **9️⃣ Tips for custom shapes**

* Power BI doesn’t have fancy drawing tools like Word.
* If you want a unique shape, create it in Word or PowerPoint.
* Then take a screenshot (Snipping Tool), save it, and insert it into Power BI as an image.

---

### 📌 **🔑 Key point:**

Text boxes, shapes, and images **don’t show data**, but they **improve instructions, look, and usability** of your report.
